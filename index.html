Learn Data PMO with Phoebe

Run the whole data portfolio - not just one project

Northgate, a 120-store retailer, stands up a data office: 30 services on the catalog - cloud, lakehouse, dashboards, KPI systems, forecasting, governance, agents - 14 people, $2,700k, one year. This course is the PMO chair for that year. A leader track gives data executives the roadmap, budget, headcount, and steering disciplines. A practitioner track runs the delivery machinery: intake and charters, critical-path timelines, sprints fitted to data work, RACI, stakeholders, conflict, EVM cost control, capacity. At its heart a live portfolio simulator: toggle the five PMO levers and watch on-time delivery climb from 1 in 10 to 9 in 10 - then press the tempting "add headcount" button and meet Brooks's law in one click. Sibling courses cover running ONE tech project and managing a programme with AI; this is the portfolio layer above both.

The leader track 🧭 · for data executives, heads of data & sponsors · 6 x 45 min · decisions, not mechanics

The steering chair: what a data PMO is and is not, the dependency-honest annual roadmap, run-vs-change budgeting, the shape and math of a 14-person data office, steering cadences that prevent month-4 surprises, and reading the portfolio health board like an executive - including when to kill, pivot, or re-baseline.

The data PMO

Project vs program vs portfolio, the 4-domain catalog as a portfolio, and what the PMO does - and pointedly does not - own. PMBOK 7's domains as the leader's checklist.

Stakeholders and steering

Portfolio health

The practitioner track 🛠️ · for PMs, delivery leads & data leads wearing the PMO hat · 10 x 45 min · method over software

One running case, built harder every session: Northgate's year-one portfolio. Intake and charters, dependency-aware timelines, sprints that respect data work's discovery risk, RACI with exactly one A, the live lever-ladder simulator (1 → 2 → 4 → 6 → 9 projects on time), stakeholders, your own calendar, conflict, EVM by hand, and the capstone: the complete year-one PMO pack.
